# THEVULGO SEO operations

## Commercial priority

1. TV mounting
2. Handyman and multi-job visits
3. IKEA and furniture assembly
4. Kitchen assembly

The homepage must keep these four services visibly above secondary categories. New location or service pages require distinct search intent, useful original content and a place in the internal-linking system. Do not publish programmatic near-duplicates.

## Monthly routine

- Review Search Console queries, pages, indexing and Core Web Vitals.
- Compare WhatsApp clicks by service and CTA location with qualified leads and won jobs.
- Refresh availability and seasonal copy in `lib/commercial.ts`.
- Check titles, descriptions, canonicals, hreflang and structured data on changed templates.
- Inspect pages with impressions but weak CTR before creating any new URL.
- Consolidate cannibalising pages and preserve equity with permanent redirects.
- Update the price catalog first; page components should read from it.

## Release checklist

- English lives at `/`; Spanish lives at `/es`. `/en` is a redirect, not a canonical destination.
- Every indexable page has one clear intent and one H1.
- No fabricated address, rating, review, availability or price is placed in structured data.
- Contextual CTA attributes include `data-event`, `data-service` and `data-cta-location`.
- Verify mobile layout, WhatsApp prefill, estimator and language alternatives.
- Run tests, lint and production build before release.

## KPI definitions

- Primary: qualified WhatsApp conversations and accepted estimates.
- Supporting: WhatsApp CTR, service-card CTR, multi-job CTA CTR, kitchen-plan CTA CTR and estimate completion.
- Diagnostic: organic landing sessions, non-brand impressions, CTR, ranking distribution and indexed canonical pages.
